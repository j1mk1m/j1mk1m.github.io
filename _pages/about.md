Hi, my name is James!

I am a Master's student (MSCS) at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by [Professor Chenyan Xiong](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~cx/). I recently graduated from CMU with a Bachelor's in Computer Science and a concentration in Algorithms and Complexity. Previously, I have worked with Professors [Daniel Fried](https://dpfried.github.io/) and [LP Morency](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~morency/) on [benchmarking AI agents as ML research assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19724).

Currently, I am a teaching assistant for [10-414 Deep Learning Systems](https://dlsyscourse.org/). During my undergrad, I was a teaching assistant for [15-251 Great Ideas in Theoretical Computer Science](https://www.cs251.com/) for 6 semesters with the last 2 semesters as Head TA.

Previously, I have worked at [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com) in Summer 24 and [MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com) in Summer 23 as a Software Development intern.

In my free time, I enjoy cooking Korean food and playing tennis!
